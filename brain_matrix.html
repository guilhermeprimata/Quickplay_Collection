HOW TO PLAY

1. Find and select numbers in ascending order from 00 up to the maximum.

2. In advanced levels with letters, clear all numbers first, then letters from A to Z.

3. MISTAKE PENALTY: Clicking the wrong tile subtracts 2x its value, un-flips your last correct tile, and shuffles the whole matrix!

4. Letter values scale: A = 10 pts, B = 20 pts... Z = 260 pts.

STRATEGY TIP
Keep your gaze near the center and scan by rows or quadrants instead of chasing each tile randomly.
