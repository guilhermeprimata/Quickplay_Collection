    ATOMIC RAID - READ ME

    Welcome to Atomic Raid! Get ready for a classic arcade experience, where your piloting and strategy skills will be tested to the limit.

    Basic Instructions

    • Movement: Use the arrow keys on your keyboard (or the virtual joystick on mobile) to move your ship.
    • Shoot: Press the spacebar (or the fire button on mobile) to shoot.
    • Barrel Roll (Evasion): Press the UP and DOWN arrows simultaneously to perform a barrel roll and evade enemy fire.
    • Fuel: Keep your fuel tank full by collecting fuel canisters that appear in the river. If you run out of fuel, you will lose a life!
    • Lives: You start with 3 lives. Avoid collisions and enemy fire to survive!
    • Upgrades: Collect points by eliminating enemies and use them in the Upgrade Shop to improve your ship!

    Advanced Tips

    • Combos: Eliminate enemies quickly to build combos and multiply your score!
    • Power-Ups: Keep an eye out for power-ups that may appear:
      • Extra Life: Gain an additional life.
      • Ammo Box: Reload your ammunition!
      • Mega Bomb: Activate a huge explosion that clears the screen.
    • Enemy Types: Each enemy has a different behavior. Learn their patterns to eliminate them more efficiently!
    • Terrain: Avoid enemy fire and terrain hazards. Bridges can be destroyed!
